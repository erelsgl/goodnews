VERSION 1
---------

The goal of this website is to reduce the toxic polarization in Israeli politics. As toxic polarization usually appears around news events, the site is going to present news articles, describing each day the news items most important to Israelis. Each news item will have four parts:

(a) The context and primary sources - with emphasis on transparent sourcing and direct attribution to avoid claims of platform bias.

(b) The opinions - from all sides of the political spectrum. All opinions will be presented in a respectable way; respect is the first key for reducing polarization.

(c) The synthesis - the various opinions should be deliberated one against the other, in order to produce a  unified statement - a statement that takes the points of truth in each opinion, and presents them to holders of the other opinions. Viewing the points of truth in other people's opinions is the second key for reducing polarization.

(d) The spirit - above the synthesized opinion, there will appear the spiritual point of view, based on the Bible and other sources that can be understood universally. This is the third key for reducing polarization: understanding we are all children of The Lord.

Generation and moderation:

(a) All news items will be generated by AI. 

(b) The AI-generated news items will pass through two distinct LLM architectures, to flag potential bias.

(c) Then, the items will be sent to the human administrator for final review.



VERSION 2
---------
The next version will introduce interactivity. Free-form unmoderated comments will not be allowed, as they are some of the most toxic elements in current news sites. Instead, there will be more structured and moderated interactivity:

(a) Lightweight micro-polls. These will be both on contents, e.g. "do you agree with X or with Y or neither?", and on form, e.g. "Did this synthesis help you understand the opposing side's logic?" to measure the platform's real-world impact on polarization.

(b) Structured delibration: instead of open text fields, require users to complete sentences such as "I agree with [Opposing Side] regarding X, but I disagree on Y", or "I agree/disagree with X because _______".  Comments will be reviewed by AI to verify that they obey the structure and the topic, as well as to filter out toxic language. 

(c) Free-form comments will be allowed only by non-anonymous users, who register by submitting their ID card. This is based on the assumption that non-anonymous users tend to think twice before submitting a false/toxic/obscene text. These, comments, too, will be moderated by AI to verify that they are on-topic and non-toxic.

To handle false-positive comment blocks, there will be an automated appeals system: a streamlined user appeal system, to avoid overwhelming the site administrator with moderation reviews. The final decisions will be reviewed by the human administrator.
